## 1. Peer-reviewed publication

**NeuralProphet for Ordinal Time Series Forecasting: A Two-Stage Approach with Threshold
Optimization**

- Yuna Han, Amira Abdul-Rahim, **Sinclair Nzenwata**, Drew Lazar (corresponding)
- Department of Mathematical Sciences, Ball State University, Muncie, IN 47306
- *Forecasting* (MDPI), 2026, **8**(6), 105: ISSN 2571-9394
- DOI: [10.3390/forecast8060105](https://doi.org/10.3390/forecast8060105)
- Received 8 Sep 2026 · Revised 1 Nov 2026 · Accepted 4 Nov 2026 · Published 7 Nov 2026 · 26 pages,
  open access
- Preprint: [preprints.org/manuscript/202610.0360](https://www.preprints.org/manuscript/202610.0360)
  (7 Oct 2026)

Two-stage ordinal forecasting: fit NeuralProphet to ordinal labels treated as continuous, then
optimise ordered thresholds on a validation segment instead of rounding naively. Evaluated on
simulations plus Beijing air quality, **electricity load in a Ball State University building**, and
U.S. Drought Monitor categories. Beats logistic and ordered-logit baselines at every horizon.

This is the single strongest credential on the site: peer-reviewed, DOI-addressable, and
undergraduate co-authorship is rare. It leads the overview page.

Filling either field puts it straight into `Person.sameAs` on every page; nothing else needs
changing.
